What to Write About

You are welcome to write about anything on the site:

  • Date corrections — if a festival or holiday date differs from your local panchangam, tell me the source and location.
  • Broken pages — a calendar grid that does not print, a link that goes nowhere, or text that does not load on mobile.
  • Content requests — a festival, temple or month you would like covered in more detail.
  • General questions — how the dates are calculated, or how to use the printable grids.

Before You Write

Please remember that this site is a personal reference project. For a binding religious decision, the final word should come from your family priest or temple; for official holidays, from the state government gazette. I can point you to the source I used, but I cannot issue a religious ruling or confirm a date for your specific location. See the about page for how the dates are sourced and the terms of use for what the site does and does not cover.
