The Tamil Calendar 2027 at a Glance

The Tamil calendar is a solar calendar followed by millions of people in Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Sri Lanka, Singapore, Malaysia and across the global Tamil diaspora. Unlike the Gregorian calendar that begins in January, the Tamil year begins in mid-April with the month of Chithirai, when the Sun enters the zodiac sign of Mesha (Aries). The year 2027 is useful for planners because it contains a full run of all twelve Tamil months: from Margazhi, which is still running at the start of January, through Thai, Masi, Panguni, Chithirai, Vaikasi, Aani, Aadi, Aavani, Purattasi, Aippasi and Karthigai, before Margazhi returns in mid-December.

Government Holidays in Tamil Nadu in 2027

The Tamil Nadu government notifies its official holiday list for each year near the end of the previous year, so always confirm against the final gazette. Based on the festival dates above and standard national holidays, the days most likely to be public holidays in Tamil Nadu in 2027 include: Pongal on 15 January, Thiruvalluvar Day on 16 January and Uzhavar Thirunal on 17 January; Republic Day on 26 January; Puthandu on 14 April; Labour Day on 1 May; Independence Day on 15 August; Vinayagar Chathurthi on 4 September; Gandhi Jayanti on 2 October; Vijayadashami on 9 October; Deepavali on 28 October; and Christmas on 25 December. Banks, schools and government offices typically close on these days, while shops and transport run on reduced schedules. Plan travel and family gatherings around the Pongal and Deepavali breaks in particular, as trains and buses are heavily booked in the days before both festivals.

Muhurtham and Auspicious Dates in 2027

Many families use the Tamil calendar 2027 to shortlist muhurtham dates — auspicious moments for weddings, housewarmings, business launches and vehicle purchases. Muhurtham timing depends on the panchangam: the positions of the Sun and Moon, the day's star (nakshatra), the tithi and the Yoga. Because these shift every year, we keep general guidance on our Tamil muhurtham dates 2027 page rather than inventing exact dates. For any important event, always cross-check with a family priest or a printed panchangam before you book venues or send invitations.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is Pongal in 2027?

Pongal falls on Friday, 15 January 2027. Bhogi is on 14 January and Mattu Pongal on 16 January 2027.

When is Tamil New Year 2027?

Puthandu, the Tamil New Year, is on Wednesday, 14 April 2027 — the first day of the Tamil month Chithirai.

Why is Tamil Deepavali on a different date from Diwali?

Tamil Nadu follows its own regional almanac tradition. In 2027, Deepavali is observed on Thursday, 28 October 2027 in Tamil Nadu, while North Indian Diwali falls on Friday, 29 October 2027 — one day later.

Which Tamil months run during the 2027 calendar year?

Calendar year 2027 spans Margazhi, Thai, Masi, Panguni, Chithirai, Vaikasi, Aani, Aadi, Aavani, Purattasi, Aippasi and Karthigai — a complete set of all twelve Tamil months.
