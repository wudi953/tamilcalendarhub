Tamil Calendar 2027 November

November 2027 is the quiet bridge between the Deepavali lights and the Karthigai Deepam flame: Aippasi ends around mid-month and the lamps month of Karthigai begins.

Tamil months: Aippasi (ஐப்பசி) till ~15 Nov · Karthigai (கார்த்திகை) from ~16 Nov
Festivals: No major festival dates this month — Karthigai month begins ~16
Holidays: No major public holidays in November 2027

Festivals and Observances in November 2027

  • Around 16 November — Karthigai month begins (கார்த்திகை மாதம்)
    The start of Karthigai opens the season of lamps: households begin adding lamps to doorways each evening, building up toward Karthigai Deepam on 12 December. In 2027 the exact start date depends on the panchangam — treat ~16 November as an approximation.
  • Karthigai Mondays and Murugan worship
    Karthigai is closely tied to Murugan and Shiva worship; temples hold special abhishekams on the star days of the month. Individual temple schedules for 2027 are announced closer to the month.

Government Holidays in November 2027

November 2027 has no major public holidays in Tamil Nadu. After the Deepavali break in October, work and school routines return to normal, and the month is often used for pre-wedding shopping and bookings ahead of the peak muhurtham season that follows Karthigai Deepam.

Auspicious Days and Notes for November 2027

Aippasi and Karthigai together form one of the most popular stretches for weddings and housewarmings in the Tamil year — the post-monsoon weather is pleasant and the almanac offers many favourable days.
