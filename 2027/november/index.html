Festivals and Observances in November 2027

  • Around 16 November — Karthigai month begins (கார்த்திகை மாதம்)
    The start of Karthigai opens the season of lamps: households begin adding lamps to doorways each evening, building up toward Karthigai Deepam on 12 December. In 2027 the exact start date depends on the panchangam — treat ~16 November as an approximation.
  • Karthigai Mondays and Murugan worship
    Karthigai is closely tied to Murugan and Shiva worship; temples hold special abhishekams on the star days of the month. Individual temple schedules for 2027 are announced closer to the month.

Government Holidays in November 2027

November 2027 has no major public holidays in Tamil Nadu. After the Deepavali break in October, work and school routines return to normal, and the month is often used for pre-wedding shopping and bookings ahead of the peak muhurtham season that follows Karthigai Deepam.

Auspicious Days and Notes for November 2027

Aippasi and Karthigai together form one of the most popular stretches for weddings and housewarmings in the Tamil year — the post-monsoon weather is pleasant and the almanac offers many favourable days. What the Tamil Months Mean

November opens in Aippasi (ஐப்பசி), the month of Deepavali and the new rice, and moves into Karthigai (கார்த்திகை) around the 16th. Karthigai is named after the Karthika star — the Pleiades — and is remembered above all as the month of lamps. The north-east monsoon is now established, the nights are cooler and the countryside is green, which makes the long rows of oil lamps lit on Karthigai Deepam feel entirely natural.

Festival Customs and Food

Karthigai Deepam is Tamil Nadu's festival of lights, observed on the full moon of Karthigai. Homes and temple courtyards are lined with small clay oil lamps lit at dusk, and at Tiruvannamalai an enormous beacon is lit atop the hill to mark the formless flame of Shiva. Families gift lamps to married daughters and neighbours, and the evening ends with crackers and a shared meal. Pongal, payasam, murukku and adhirasam are typical, and temples distribute lamp oil and wicks as prasad. The preceding days include lamp-making and kolam competitions in many neighbourhoods.

Season and Farming

The north-east monsoon is at its peak, and the main harvest begins in right-handed earnest. Farmers cut paddy, dry the grain on village threshing floors and fill the tanks that will carry the land into the dry months. Vegetables from the rain-fed crop are plentiful, and the cooler weather allows full days of field work. The combination of a full granary and a mild season is why Karthigai is remembered as a contented month — the hard decisions about next year's sowing can wait until after the lamps are lit.

Travel and Temple Notes

Tiruvannamalai is the month's biggest draw: on Deepam night, huge crowds circle the hill to see the beacon, and roads and parking fill from the afternoon. Arrive early, expect walking distances and follow the police diversions. In Chennai and other cities, apartment courtyards and temple streets become candlelit in the evening, which is beautiful but slow-moving for traffic. Monsoon showers remain possible, so carry an umbrella and protect electrical decorations. The weeks after Deepam are a popular window for final muhurtham events of the year.

Born This Month: Star and Temperament

Those born after the Karthigai transition belong to Vrischika (Scorpio, விருச்சிகம்), a rashi traditionally described as intense, resilient and private. Vrischika natives are often said to commit deeply, to notice what others overlook and to recover strongly from setbacks — qualities that suit the fixed flame of the month's festival. The Aippasi half falls under Thula (Libra), associated with balance and sociability. These solar-rashi descriptions are only a starting point; the birth nakshatra and the full chart give a much more precise reading.
