How to Print or Save as PDF

Step 1: open the month you want from the month index. Step 2: press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Cmd+P (Mac). Step 3: in the print dialog, choose "Save as PDF" as the destination to get a Tamil calendar 2027 PDF file, or pick your printer for a paper copy. Step 4: enable "Background graphics" in the print options so the festival highlights and colours appear. Each page is laid out to fit a single A4 sheet in portrait orientation.

Tips for best results: print at 90–100% scale; the calendar grid and festival list are sized for that range. If your browser clips the table, use "Scale: Fit to page width". For a year-long wall calendar, print all twelve months and bind them at the top edge — each month page carries its festivals and holidays, so you never need a second sheet.

What Each Monthly PDF Page Includes

Every monthly page contains: the full Gregorian grid for that month of 2027 with festivals highlighted in gold and national holidays marked in red; the Tamil month names (in English and Tamil script) that run through the month; the complete list of festival dates with weekdays; expected government holidays; and notes on auspicious days. Eight deep-dive festival pages — Pongal, Thai Pusam, Tamil New Year, Vinayagar Chathurthi, Deepavali, Navratri, Karthigai Deepam and Aadi Perukku — also print cleanly if you want a customs-and-wishes reference at home.

Note: all dates on this site follow Indian Standard Time (IST), calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary slightly. Always confirm with a local panchangam.

Two Ways to Get Your Copy

The ready-made PDF above collects the whole year in one file — convenient for sharing by email or WhatsApp, and for keeping a single copy on your phone or tablet. For one particular month, printing straight from the page is usually better: the output always matches the latest version of that page, so a corrected festival date never leaves an outdated copy on your wall.
