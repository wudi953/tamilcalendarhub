Tamil Calendar 2027 September

September 2027 opens the festive second half of the year: Vinayagar Chathurthi on 4 September and Navarathiri beginning 30 September, as Aavani hands over to Purattasi.

Festivals in September 2027

  • Saturday, 4 September — Vinayagar Chathurthi (விநாயகர் சதுர்த்தி)
    The birthday of Lord Ganesha. Clay Vinayagar idols are installed in homes and street pandals, worshipped through the day with kozhukattai and ellu urundai, and later immersed in water. Neighbourhood processions run through the evening.
  • Wednesday, 30 September — Navarathiri begins (நவராத்திரி)
    The nine-night festival of the Goddess starts on this day, running through 8 October with Vijayadashami on 9 October. Families set up the Golu display of dolls and invite guests for sundal and thamboolam. Full Navratri 2027 guide →

Government Holidays in September 2027

Vinayagar Chathurthi on Saturday, 4 September is expected to be a Tamil Nadu government holiday; falling on a Saturday, many organisations treat it as a normal weekend. Navarathiri's start on 30 September is not a holiday by itself. Confirm the final state gazette when published.

Auspicious Days and Notes for September 2027

Purattasi is traditionally a month of Venkateswara worship — many devotees observe Purattasi Saturdays with vegetarian food and special offerings, and some fast through the month. Because of these observances, Purattasi is generally avoided for weddings, while the Aavani days before mid-September carry the year's wedding-season momentum.
