Tamil Calendar 2027 October

October 2027 is a double-header: Navarathiri ends with Vijayadashami on 9 October, and the lights of Deepavali arrive on 28 October — plus Gandhi Jayanti on the 2nd.

Tamil months: Purattasi (புரட்டாசி) till ~16 Oct · Aippasi (ஐப்பசி) from ~17 Oct
Festivals: Vijayadashami 9 · Deepavali 28 (TN)
National holiday: Gandhi Jayanti — 2 October (Saturday)
Sun sign: Kanya (Virgo) · Thula (Libra) from mid-month

Festivals in October 2027

  • Saturday, 9 October — Vijayadashami / Ayudha Puja (விஜயதசமி)
    The tenth and final day of Navarathiri. Vehicles, machines, tools and books are cleaned and decorated for Ayudha Puja, and the day is the traditional start for new learning — music classes and dance schools admit fresh students in droves. Navratri 2027 guide →
  • Thursday, 28 October — Deepavali (தீபாவளி)
    The festival of lights as observed in Tamil Nadu: an oil bath before sunrise, new clothes, sweets, and crackers through the day. Note that North Indian Diwali falls a day later, on Friday, 29 October 2027. Full Deepavali 2027 guide →

Government Holidays in October 2027

Gandhi Jayanti on Saturday, 2 October is a national holiday. Vijayadashami on 9 October and Deepavali on 28 October are expected Tamil Nadu holidays — Deepavali usually brings a one- or two-day official break, and many companies extend it across the week. Train and bus bookings for the Deepavali weekend open early and sell out fast, so plan hometown travel as soon as dates are confirmed.

Auspicious Days and Notes for October 2027

Vijayadashami is one of the most auspicious days of the entire year — new ventures, new classes and first lessons (vidyarambam) are traditionally begun on this day, so it also sees heavy demand for muhurtham slots. After Deepavali, Aippasi carries the momentum into the wedding season that peaks in the following months. See our muhurtham dates guide for how auspicious timings are determined.

Note: all dates on this page follow Indian Standard Time (IST), calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary slightly. Always confirm with a local panchangam.
