Tamil Calendar 2027 December

December 2027 closes the year in grand style: Karthigai Deepam on 12 December, Christmas on 25 December, and the devotional month of Margazhi beginning around the 16th.

Festivals: Karthigai Deepam — 12 December (Sunday)
Holidays: Christmas — 25 December (national holiday)

Festivals in December 2027

  • Sunday, 12 December — Karthigai Deepam (கார்த்திகை தீபம்)
    The festival of lamps. Rows of clay lamps glow on doorsteps, and at Tiruvannamalai the great Bharani Deepam flame is lit atop Arunachala hill before huge crowds. Full Karthigai Deepam 2027 guide →
  • Around 16 December — Margazhi month begins (மார்கழி மாதம்)
    The most devotional month of the Tamil year: Andal's Thiruppavai is recited each morning, Margazhi kolam line the streets, and Chennai's famous music and dance season runs in full swing. Exact start date depends on the panchangam.

Government Holidays in December 2027

Christmas on Saturday, 25 December is a national holiday across India, and the days around it are popular for family trips and beach holidays in Tamil Nadu. Karthigai Deepam itself is widely observed in Tamil Nadu; check the state gazette for its exact holiday status in 2027.

Auspicious Days and Notes for December 2027

Early December — before Margazhi begins around the 16th — is often used for final muhurtham events of the year, as Margazhi itself is traditionally avoided for weddings. Many families instead use Margazhi for prayers, temple visits and planning the year ahead, with muhurtham activity resuming after Thai begins in mid-January. See our muhurtham dates guide.

Note: all dates on this page follow Indian Standard Time (IST), calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary slightly. Always confirm with a local panchangam.
