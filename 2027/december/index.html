Festivals in December 2027

  • Sunday, 12 December — Karthigai Deepam (கார்த்திகை தீபம்)
    The festival of lamps. Rows of clay lamps glow on doorsteps, and at Tiruvannamalai the great Bharani Deepam flame is lit atop Arunachala hill before huge crowds. Full Karthigai Deepam 2027 guide →
  • Around 16 December — Margazhi month begins (மார்கழி மாதம்)
    The most devotional month of the Tamil year: Andal's Thiruppavai is recited each morning, Margazhi kolam line the streets, and Chennai's famous music and dance season runs in full swing. Exact start date depends on the panchangam.

Government Holidays in December 2027

Christmas on Saturday, 25 December is a national holiday across India, and the days around it are popular for family trips and beach holidays in Tamil Nadu. Karthigai Deepam itself is widely observed in Tamil Nadu; check the state gazette for its exact holiday status in 2027.

Auspicious Days and Notes for December 2027

Early December — before Margazhi begins around the 16th — is often used for final muhurtham events of the year, as Margazhi itself is traditionally avoided for weddings. Many families instead use Margazhi for prayers, temple visits and planning the year ahead, with muhurtham activity resuming after Thai begins in mid-January. See our muhurtham dates guide.

Note: all dates on this page follow Indian Standard Time (IST), calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary slightly. Always confirm with a local panchangam.

What the Tamil Months Mean

December begins in Karthigai (கார்த்திகை), the lamp month, and crosses into Margazhi (மார்கழி) around the 16th. Margazhi derives from the idea of the "right path" and is the most devotional month of the Tamil year. It is a season of early-morning bhajans, temple processions and the celebrated Margazhi music season in Chennai, when concert halls across the city run back-to-back performances. The calendar is closing, but the mood is inward and disciplined rather than celebratory.

Festival Customs and Food

Margazhi has no single large festival; its observance is daily. Devotees rise before dawn, bathe, light lamps and sing hymns — the famous Tiruppavai and Tiruvembavai verses are recited in homes and temples. Many temples hold Brahmotsavam processions with car festivals, and households keep a simple vegetarian diet through the month. Food is warming and modest: pongal, payasam, curd rice and sesame-based sweets. On Thiruvathirai, families prepare special rice dishes and fast, and the month ends with the Bhogi bonfire that opens the Pongal season in January.

Season and Farming

This is the cool, dry stretch after the north-east monsoon. Harvesting is largely complete, grain is stored and fields are left to rest before the next cycle. Farmers repair irrigation works and decide on seed for the coming year, while the weather stays mild enough for outdoor work without the pressure of rain. In Chennai, the cool mornings and clear evenings are exactly what make the long music and dance season possible — the cultural calendar and the agricultural one are quietly in step.

Travel and Temple Notes

Margazhi temple visits begin before sunrise, so plan around very early starts and dress warmly for the pre-dawn chill. Chennai's music season fills auditoriums and local trains in the evenings, and accommodation during the festival weeks should be booked early. Car festivals draw large crowds and can close roads for hours. Margazhi is traditionally avoided for weddings and housewarmings, so the muhurtham calendar is quiet; the final auspicious window of the year falls in early December, before the month begins. Confirm any temple timings locally, as processions vary between shrines.

Born This Month: Star and Temperament

Those born after the Margazhi transition belong to Dhanu (Sagittarius, தனுசு), a rashi traditionally linked with optimism, curiosity and a love of learning and travel. Dhanu natives are often described as frank, generous and restless — more interested in the larger picture than in small details, and comfortable with change. The Karthigai days at the start of the month fall under Vrischika (Scorpio), associated with intensity and determination. As always, these solar-rashi sketches are broad; the birth star and the moon's placement carry the real weight in a horoscope.
