Tamil Muhurtham Dates 2027

Planning a wedding, housewarming or business launch in 2027? Here is how muhurtham dates are actually determined — and why you should confirm any date with a panchangam before booking.

What Makes a Day a Muhurtham Day

A muhurtham is a precise window of time — not just a date — judged favourable by five factors of the panchangam: the nakshatra (the Moon's star), the tithi (lunar day), the vaara (weekday), the yoga, and the lagna (the rising zodiac sign), which divides a single day into different favourable windows of one to two hours each. Two weddings can fall on the same date with entirely different muhurtham timings. This is why we do not print a list of "lucky dates" here: the same day can be excellent for one family and unsuitable for another depending on horoscopes and the specific lagna required for the ceremony.

The Auspicious Rhythm of the Tamil Year 2027

While exact dates belong to the panchangam, the Tamil year has a well-known rhythm that planners use. The months of Thai (mid-January to mid-February), Chithirai–Vaikasi (mid-April to mid-June), Aavani–Purattasi edges (mid-August to mid-September) and Aippasi–Karthigai (mid-October to mid-December) are traditionally favoured for weddings and housewarmings. The months of Aadi (mid-July to mid-August) and Margazhi (mid-December to mid-January) are customarily avoided for weddings, though Aadi Perukku itself and Vijayadashami are auspicious single days.

Three anchor days of 2027 stand out for new beginnings, drawn from our festival table: Vijayadashami on 9 October — the classic day for vidyarambam and new ventures; Puthandu on 14 April — the fresh start of the year; and Aadi Perukku on 3 August — the traditional pick of the Aadi month. Days of festivals from our table, such as Panguni Uthiram on 22 March, are also considered highly auspicious for prayers related to marriage.

How to Confirm Your 2027 Muhurtham Date

Step one: shortlist a period using the monthly pages on this site — January through December — to avoid festival-travel clashes. Step two: consult a printed 2027 panchangam or a trusted online almanac for the nakshatra and tithi of your candidate dates. Step three: sit with your family priest or a recognised astrologer, who will match the day against horoscopes and fix the lagna — the exact two-hour window within the day. Only then should venues and caterers be confirmed, since premium muhurtham windows in popular months (Aippasi and Karthigai, especially) are booked up to a year ahead.

Muhurtham for Different Occasions

Weddings (kalyanam): the most demanding muhurtham, matched to both horoscopes; popular windows cluster in Chithirai–Vaikasi and Aippasi–Karthigai. Housewarming (griha pravesham): generally flexible beyond the cautious months; many families prefer days close to full moon. Business launches and new accounts: Vijayadashami and Pongal-season days are perennial favourites. Vehicle purchases: often done on festival days itself, when dealers offer special pujas. For each of these, the weekday, star and lagna requirements differ — another reason a one-size-fits-all date list is unreliable.

Note: all dates on this page follow Indian Standard Time (IST), calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary slightly. Always confirm with a local panchangam and your family priest.
