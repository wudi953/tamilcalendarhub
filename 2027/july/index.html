Festivals and Observances in July 2027

  • Around 16 July — Aadi month begins (ஆடி மாதம்)
    Aadi is the month of the Goddess. Every Friday is special: women light lamps, offer kozhukattai and pay respect to Amman shrines, and the Aadi 1 temple rollover is marked with special pujas. Fridays on the 16th, 23rd and 30th are the natural focus of the month's worship.
  • Aadi temple fairs
    Amman temples across Tamil Nadu hold flag-hoisting festivals and multi-day fairs through Aadi — each temple announces its own schedule, so check locally.

Government Holidays in July 2027

July 2027 has no major public holidays in Tamil Nadu. Aadi Fridays draw worshippers but are not government holidays; devotees typically visit temples before work. For businesses that depend on temple-town footfall, the Aadi fair season is actually one of the busier stretches of the year.

Auspicious Days and Notes for July 2027

Traditional practice treats Aadi as a cautious month for weddings and new business launches — many families simply avoid scheduling them here and use the month for Amman worship instead. Gold purchases and property registrations also tend to slow down until Aadi Perukku and Aavani arrive in August. For precise timings later in the year, start from our muhurtham dates guide.

Note: all dates on this page follow Indian Standard Time (IST), calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary slightly. Always confirm with a local panchangam.

What the Tamil Months Mean

July begins in Aani (ஆனி), the hot "ornament" month, and crosses into Aadi (ஆடி) around the 17th. Aadi takes its name from the idea of a beginning or root and is associated with the Asini nakshatra. It is the month when the south-west monsoon usually arrives, rivers swell and the agricultural year effectively restarts. Traditionally a cautious month for weddings, Aadi is nonetheless one of the most emotionally significant months in Tamil family life because of its river festival.

Festival Customs and Food

Aadi Perukku, usually on the 18th of Aadi, celebrates the rising water rather than a deity. Women gather at the riverbank with new rice, flowers, turmeric and a painted kolam on the sand, offer prayers for a good harvest, and then share food; the day marks the point when the river is believed to be at its most fertile. Aadi Velli, the Fridays of the month, are dedicated to Amman, with lamps, kolams and simple vegetarian meals. New rice pongal, payasam and sundal are the characteristic dishes, and many families avoid non-vegetarian food on Fridays.

Season and Farming

This is the start of the south-west monsoon and the main sowing window for the kuruvai crop. Fields are ploughed and seed is sown as soon as the rains arrive, so the timing of the first showers is watched anxiously. Rivers and tanks fill, irrigation channels are reopened and the delta turns green within weeks. For farmers, Aadi is the opposite of the idle heat of June: it is the busiest stretch of the year, and the saying that the month's water is gold is taken quite literally.

Travel and Temple Notes

Aadi Perukku brings large crowds to riverbanks in Tiruchirappalli, Madurai, Thanjavur and Chennai's Adyar and Cooum areas. Go early in the morning, keep a safe distance from the water — the monsoon can raise levels quickly — and expect traffic near the ghats. Aadi is traditionally avoided for weddings and housewarmings, so the wedding calendar is quieter this month. Temple visits are nevertheless busy on Fridays, when Amman shrines fill with women offering lamps and flowers. Carry an umbrella; sudden showers are normal.

Born This Month: Star and Temperament

Those born after the Aadi transition belong to Kataka (Cancer, கடகம்) in the solar-rashi system. Kataka is traditionally described as home-centred, sensitive and protective — people who remember details, care deeply for family and prefer familiar surroundings. It is a watery sign, which fits a month defined by rivers and rain. The Aani half of the month falls under Mithuna (Gemini), associated with curiosity and quick thinking. These are broad descriptions; a full horoscope depends far more on the birth star and moon.
