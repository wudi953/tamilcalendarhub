Tamil Calendar 2027 May

May 2027 is early summer in Tamil Nadu — Chithirai hands over to Vaikasi, temple chariot season is in full swing, and school summer holidays begin across the state.

Festivals in May 2027

  • Around mid-May — Vaikasi Visakam (வைகாசி விசாகம்)
    Vaikasi Visakam celebrates the birthday of Lord Murugan, observed when the Visakam star falls in the month of Vaikasi. Special abhishekams and processions are held at Murugan temples including Palani and Thiruchendur. The exact date in 2027 depends on the panchangam — check your local temple notice or almanac, as the star day shifts year to year.
  • Chithirai festival season continues
    The famous Madurai Chithirai festival, culminating in the celestial wedding of Meenakshi and the Ther Thiruvizha chariot procession, often stretches into early May. Confirm the 2027 schedule with temple announcements if you plan to attend.

Government Holidays in May 2027

Labour Day on Saturday, 1 May is a national holiday across India, including Tamil Nadu. Beyond that, May 2027 has no other major public holidays, though school summer vacation runs through the month, making it a popular time for family trips and temple tourism. Hill stations like Ooty, Kodaikanal and Yercaud see peak crowds, so book accommodation well in advance.

Auspicious Days and Notes for May 2027

Vaikasi is traditionally considered a favourable month for weddings and new ventures, and the post-Puthandu momentum often carries into May for muhurtham events. Heat is intense across the plains, so morning-hour events are preferred. For exact muhurtham timings in May 2027, consult a panchangam and your family priest — our muhurtham dates guide explains the factors involved, from nakshatra to tithi.

Note: all dates on this page follow Indian Standard Time (IST), calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary slightly. Always confirm with a local panchangam.
