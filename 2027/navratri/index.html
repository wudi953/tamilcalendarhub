Navratri 2027 (Navarathiri)

The nine nights of the Goddess run from Wednesday, 30 September to Friday, 8 October 2027, closing with Vijayadashami on Saturday, 9 October — Golu season in every Tamil home.

Starts: Wednesday, 30 September 2027
Ends (Vijayadashami): Saturday, 9 October 2027
Tamil name: நவராத்திரி (Navarathiri)
Key custom: Golu doll display & sundal distribution

Navratri 2027 Dates

Navarathiri 2027 begins on Wednesday, 30 September and runs for nine nights through Friday, 8 October, with Vijayadashami on Saturday, 9 October 2027 marking the victory of good over evil. Each night honours one form of the Goddess: the first three nights for Durga (courage), the next three for Lakshmi (prosperity) and the final three for Saraswati (wisdom). Saraswati Puja falls on the eighth or ninth day each year — check your local panchangam for the exact 2027 date — and Ayudha Puja on Vijayadashami eve sees vehicles, machines and tools washed and decorated.

Golu and How Navarathiri Is Celebrated

The signature Tamil custom of Navarathiri is the Golu: a stepped display of dolls and figurines arranged on odd-numbered tiers in the living room, telling stories from mythology, village life and increasingly modern themes. Families add one new doll each year, pass sets down generations, and invite friends and neighbours home throughout the nine nights. Guests are offered sundal — boiled chickpeas, black-eyed peas or kidney beans tempered with coconut and curry leaves — along with thamboolam, a return gift of betel leaves, turmeric, a coconut and a small mirror or comb for women.

Each evening, a lamp and kumkum are offered to the Goddess, and devotional songs fill the neighbourhood. In Mylapore and other Chennai neighbourhoods, Golu-hopping is a social season of its own. On the ninth day, Saraswati Puja places books and musical instruments before the Goddess, and on Vijayadashami children begin new learning — the classic day for the first music lesson or the first syllable written on rice. Temple cities like Kanyakumari, where the goddess is honoured across the nine days, see their biggest crowds of the season.

Navratri Wishes in Tamil (நவராத்திரி வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

நவராத்திரி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Navarathiri Nalvazhthukkal — "Happy Navratri!"

அம்மன் அருள் நிறைந்த நவராத்திரி!

Amman arul niraaindha Navarathiri — "May Navratri be filled with the Goddess's grace."

விஜயதசமி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள் — வெற்றி நல்லது செய்யட்டும்!

Vijayadashami nalvazhthukkal — "Happy Vijayadashami; may victory bring good to all!"

Government Holiday Notes for Navratri 2027

The start of Navarathiri on 30 September 2027 is not itself a public holiday in Tamil Nadu, but Vijayadashami on Saturday, 9 October is expected to be a state holiday. Saraswati Puja and Ayudha Puja days often see half-day or optional leave in schools and offices. Confirm the final state gazette when published.

Auspicious Notes for Vijayadashami

Vijayadashami is among the most sought-after days of the Tamil year for new beginnings: vidyarambam (a child's first letters), music and dance admissions, business launches and even wedding muhurthams cluster around this day.
