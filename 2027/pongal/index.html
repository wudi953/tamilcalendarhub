Pongal 2027

The four-day harvest festival of Tamil Nadu: Bhogi on 14 January, Pongal on 15 January, Mattu Pongal on 16 January — and Thai Pusam a week later on 22 January.

Date: Friday, 15 January 2027 (Thai 1)
Tamil name: தைப்பொங்கல் (Thai Pongal)
Season: Bhogi 14 · Pongal 15 · Mattu Pongal 16 · Thai Pusam 22
Expected holidays: 15–17 January (TN)

All dates in IST, calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary.

Pongal 2027 Date and Time

Pongal falls on Friday, 15 January 2027, the first day of the Tamil month Thai, when the Sun enters Makara (Capricorn). The main ritual — cooking the Pongal pot — is traditionally done in the auspicious part of the morning, after sunrise and the early puja; families set up the decorated clay pot in the courtyard or verandah and boil newly harvested rice with jaggery and milk until it boils over with the cry "Pongalo Pongal!". Most celebrations run from sunrise through the morning; there is no single fixed "muhurtham minute" for households, though priests may suggest a specific window each year from the panchangam.

How Pongal Is Celebrated

Pongal is the thanksgiving of the Tamil farming year, celebrated over four days. On Bhogi (14 January), houses are cleaned and painted and old belongings are burnt before sunrise on a small bonfire — a symbolic casting away of the old. On Pongal day itself (15 January), the household cooks the new rice: the decorated pot, often tied with fresh turmeric and ginger saplings, is set over a fire in the open, and the moment the milk boils over is greeted with joy as a sign of prosperity. Surya, the Sun god, is offered the first share.

On Mattu Pongal (16 January), the cattle that ploughed the fields are bathed, their horns painted, and they are fed the first Pongal of the day. In villages around Madurai, Trichy and beyond, Jallikattu — the ancient bull-embracing sport — draws huge crowds. The season closes with Kanum Pongal, a day of family outings, when leftover rice is offered to birds and the remaining days are spent visiting relatives. Sugarcane stacks, mango-leaf thoranams and freshly drawn kolam make the whole state look freshly washed.

Pongal Wishes in Tamil (பொங்கல் வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

பொங்கலோ பொங்கல்! இனிய தைப்பொங்கல் நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Pongalo Pongal! Iniya Thaipongal Nalvazhthukkal — "May this Pongal bring you a happy Thai Pongal!"

நல்ல அறுவடை, நிறைவான ஆண்டு நிறைய வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Nalla aruvadai, niraivaana aandu niraiva vaazhthukkal — "Wishing you a good harvest and a fulfilling year."

தை பிறந்தால் வழி பிறக்கும்

Thai pirandhal vazhi pirakkum — the classic proverb: "When Thai is born, a way opens" — the belief that the Thai month brings solutions and fresh beginnings.

Government Holiday Notes for Pongal 2027

Pongal (15 January), Thiruvalluvar Day (16 January) and Uzhavar Thirunal (17 January) are expected Tamil Nadu holidays, giving a long weekend stretch — especially welcome since Pongal itself falls on a Friday in 2027. Schools, banks and most government offices close; confirm the final gazette. Travel is heavy: reserve buses and trains well before mid-January.
