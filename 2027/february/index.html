Festivals in February 2027

  • Saturday, 20 February — Masi Magam (மாசி மகம்)
    Masi Magam falls on the day the Magam star aligns with the full moon in the Tamil month of Masi. Temple deities are carried in procession to the seashore, a river or a temple tank, and devotees take a ritual dip alongside them. The famous Mahamaham tank at Kumbakonam is closely associated with Masi celebrations, and coastal towns from Chennai to Rameswaram hold sea-bathing processions. Families believe the bath washes away accumulated negativity, and offerings are made to ancestors on this day. In Sri Lanka, Singapore and Malaysia, Murugan and Shiva temples hold parallel ceremonies, so diaspora families often plan temple visits around this weekend. Arrive early — processions usually begin in the morning and conclude around midday.

Government Holidays in February 2027

February 2027 has no major public holidays in Tamil Nadu — it is one of the calmest months of the year for work and school schedules. Banks and offices follow normal hours throughout. If you are planning temple visits for Masi Magam on Saturday the 20th, no leave is needed since it falls on a weekend, though crowds at popular coastal temples will be significant from early morning.

Auspicious Days and Notes for February 2027

Masi is traditionally associated with Shiva worship — the great Masi legend links the month to Shiva's appearance as a column of fire. Devotees often observe Shiva-related vows on Mondays of the month, and temple abhishekams are common. Because Thai ends around the 13th, the first half of February still carries the auspicious momentum of Thai, which many families use for engagements and property registrations before Masi begins.
