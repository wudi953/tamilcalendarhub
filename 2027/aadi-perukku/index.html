Aadi Perukku 2027

The 18th day of Aadi — Tuesday, 3 August 2027 — when families gather on the banks of the Cauvery to thank the rivers that feed their fields and homes.

Date: Tuesday, 3 August 2027 (Aadi 18)
Tamil name: ஆடிப் பெருக்கு (Aadi Perukku)
Meaning: "Perukku" = rising/flooding of rivers
Iconic spot: Kallanai, the Grand Anicut on the Cauvery

All dates in IST, calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary.

Aadi Perukku 2027 Date and Time

Aadi Perukku falls on Tuesday, 3 August 2027, the eighteenth day of the Aadi month. The rituals are tied to daylight hours: families reach the riverbank in the morning, cook the ceremonial rice dishes on the spot or bring them prepared, and offer them to the river along with flowers and lamps through the morning and early afternoon. There is no single fixed minute — the morning hours are the traditional window, and temples along the river hold special abhishekams and archanas through the day.

How Aadi Perukku Is Celebrated

Aadi Perukku celebrates the monsoon-fed rise of the rivers — above all the Cauvery — that sustain Tamil agriculture. On the 18th day of Aadi, families travel to riverbanks, temple ghats and even the seashore in towns without rivers. The heart of the ritual is the preparation of special rice dishes, each with a meaning: coconut rice for prosperity, sweet pongal for sweetness in life, lemon rice, curd rice and tamarind rice for the everyday needs of a household, all offered to the river goddess before being shared as prasadam.

Women fast through the morning and pray for the wellbeing of their families, and newly married couples come together to the riverbank in their first Aadi — the day is considered especially blessing-filled for young households hoping for children and abundance. Amman temples hold kappu (protection) ceremonies through the month, and gold and silver dealers see steady demand as families buy something auspicious for the day. At Kallanai, the 2,000-year-old Grand Anicut, the crowds are at their largest; smaller rivers like the Vaigai in Madurai and the Thamirabarani in the south hold parallel celebrations.

Aadi Perukku Wishes in Tamil (ஆடிப் பெருக்கு வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

ஆடிப் பெருக்கு நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Aadi Perukku Nalvazhthukkal — "Happy Aadi Perukku!"

ஆறு பெருக வாழ்க, குடும்பம் பெருக வாழ்க!

Aaru peruga vaazhga, kudumbam peruga vaazhga — "May the river rise, and may the family flourish with it."

நிறைவான மழை, நிறைவான அறுவடை!

Niraivaana mazhai, niraivaana aruvadai — "May the rains be full, and the harvest be full!"

Government Holiday Notes for Aadi Perukku 2027

Aadi Perukku on Tuesday, 3 August 2027 is not generally a public holiday in Tamil Nadu, though families in the Cauvery delta districts often take the day off to attend the riverside rituals. Independence Day follows on Sunday, 15 August. Confirm local announcements for any district-specific holidays.

Auspicious Notes for Aadi Perukku

While Aadi is traditionally a cautious month for weddings, Aadi Perukku itself is one of the auspicious moments of the month — riverbank engagements and new ventures for farming families are common on this day.
