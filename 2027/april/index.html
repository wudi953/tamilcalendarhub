Festivals in April 2027

  • Wednesday, 14 April — Puthandu / Tamil New Year (புத்தாண்டு)
    Puthandu marks the first day of Chithirai and the start of the new Tamil year. Homes are decorated with elaborate kolam, doorways are hung with mango leaves, and families arrange the kanni — a tray of fruits, gold, betel leaves and a mirror — to be viewed first thing in the morning for good luck. Temples hold special pujas, and the day is a public holiday in Tamil Nadu. In Sri Lanka, Singapore and Malaysia the same day is celebrated as Sinhala–Tamil New Year. Full Tamil New Year 2027 page →
  • 13 April — Chithirai eve
    The last day of Panguni is often spent cleaning and decorating the home ahead of the new year, and shops do brisk business in new clothes, mango leaves and sweets.

Government Holidays in April 2027

Puthandu on Wednesday, 14 April is expected to be a Tamil Nadu government holiday; many companies also give 13 April off, creating a long new-year break. Schools frequently schedule term holidays through April and May. Because the holiday falls midweek, plan travel between 12 and 16 April for the best stretch — trains toward Madurai, Trichy and hometowns across the state fill up early.

Auspicious Days and Notes for April 2027

Chithirai is one of the most auspicious Tamil months of the year — the fresh start of Puthandu makes the weeks that follow popular for property registrations, business openings and wedding planning. The famous Chithirai festival of Madurai, honouring Meenakshi's celestial wedding, runs in this season; confirm its 2027 schedule with the temple or local announcements.
