Deepavali 2027

Deepavali falls on Thursday, 28 October 2027 in Tamil Nadu — one day before North Indian Diwali on 29 October — with the ritual oil bath before sunrise and lamps after dusk.

Date (Tamil Nadu): Thursday, 28 October 2027
North India (Diwali): Friday, 29 October 2027
Tamil name: தீபாவளி (Deepavali)
Expected holiday: 28 October (TN)

All dates in IST, calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary.

Deepavali 2027 Date and Time

In Tamil Nadu, Deepavali is observed on Thursday, 28 October 2027, following the Tamil almanac tradition that fixes the festival by the Aswayuja month and the Naraka Chathurdasi tithi. The most important timing of the day is the ritual oil bath (ganga snanam), taken before sunrise — elders apply oil to the younger members of the family before the bath, and everyone then dresses in new clothes. Crackers and celebrations run through the day, and lamps are lit after dusk. North Indian Diwali, based on the Amavasya (new moon), falls on Friday, 29 October 2027, so families with roots in different regions sometimes celebrate on both days.

Why Tamil Deepavali Differs from Diwali

The Tamil almanac (panchangam) computes the festival differently from the North Indian Amavasya-based reckoning, and in most years — including 2027 — Tamil Nadu celebrates a day earlier. Both festivals honour the same core story: the defeat of the demon Narakasura by Krishna and Satyabhama, remembered in Tamil tradition as Naraka Chathurdasi, the day the tyrant was vanquished and the world freed from fear. The one-day difference is a long-standing regional custom, not a disagreement: Tamil families follow the date announced in the Tamil panchangam and by local temple authorities.

How Deepavali Is Celebrated in Tamil Nadu

The Tamil Deepavali day starts before dawn. Elders apply gingelly (sesame) oil to the heads of the younger family members, followed by the hot water bath — often with a packet of shikakai powder. After the bath comes the first bite: a piece of medicine-like lehiyam, a herb-and-jaggery paste that elders prepare to protect the stomach through a day of sweets and fried snacks, and the morning is spent in new clothes, temple visits and meeting neighbours.

Sweets and savouries are prepared days ahead: adhirasam, mixture, thattai, murukku and the classic Deepavali legiyam. Crackers begin softly in the morning and build to a thunderous evening, and in the cities fire-safety advisories accompany the season. Many families also perform a Lakshmi puja or a special puja at their family temple, and it is customary to visit elders and receive their blessings first thing after the bath. For businesses, Deepavali is also a natural milestone for closing old accounts and blessing new account books.

Deepavali Wishes in Tamil (தீபாவளி வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

தீபாவளி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Deepavali Nalvazhthukkal — "Happy Deepavali!"

தீபாவளி நல்லது செய்யட்டும், ஒளி நிறைந்த ஆண்டாக அமையட்டும்!

Deepavali nalladhu seyyattum, oli niraaindha aandu aghaiyattum — "May Deepavali bring good, and may the year be filled with light."

ஒளியும் மகிழ்ச்சியும் நிறைந்த தீபாவளி!

Oliyum magizhchiyum niraaindha Deepavali — "A Deepavali filled with light and joy!"

Government Holiday Notes for Deepavali 2027

Deepavali on Thursday, 28 October 2027 is expected to be a Tamil Nadu government holiday, and most companies add Friday, 29 October to bridge into the weekend — creating a four-day break. Confirm the official gazette when notified. Travel peaks: buses and trains to hometowns sell out weeks ahead, and firecracker markets like Sattur and Sivakasi see their busiest weeks.

Auspicious Notes Around Deepavali

Deepavali sits in the Aippasi month, one of the favoured stretches for weddings and housewarmings in the Tamil year.
