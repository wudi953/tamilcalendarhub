Tamil New Year 2027 (Puthandu)

Puthandu — the Tamil New Year — falls on Wednesday, 14 April 2027, opening the month of Chithirai with fresh kolam, the kanni tray and temple visits across Tamil Nadu and the diaspora.

Date: Wednesday, 14 April 2027 (Chithirai 1)
Tamil name: புத்தாண்டு (Puthandu) · தமிழ் புத்தாண்டு
Also called: Varusha Pirappu · Chithirai 1
Expected holiday: 14 April (TN government holiday)

All dates in IST, calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary.

Puthandu 2027 Date and Time

Puthandu falls on Wednesday, 14 April 2027, the day the Sun enters Mesha (Aries) and the first day of Chithirai. Temple pujas typically begin at sunrise, and families complete the kanni ritual early in the morning before other celebrations. There is no single minute-level muhurtham for the household celebration; the day itself is the auspicious window, and priests may announce a specific puja timing for each temple from the panchangam. In Sri Lanka, Singapore and Malaysia, the same day is celebrated as the Sinhala–Tamil New Year, with similar morning rituals and family feasts.

How Puthandu Is Celebrated

The most distinctive Puthandu ritual is the kanni. The night before, families arrange a tray with fruits, betel leaves, gold or silver, a mirror, flowers and a lit lamp, keeping it beside a decorated doorway. At dawn, everyone views the kanni first thing — before anything else — as an omen of abundance for the year. Doorways are framed with mango-leaf thoranam, and elaborate kolam are drawn in the courtyard with rice flour, often featuring the year in figures or auspicious symbols.

Temple visits follow: major Shiva and Amman temples hold special pujas, and in many households the day begins with a new almanac (panchangam) being read aloud — a custom called panchanga pathanam. The traditional feast is heavy on sweet-and-bitter balance: mango pachadi, combining raw mango, jaggery and neem flowers, symbolically reminds everyone to take the years sweet and bitter alike. New clothes, gifts for elders and children, and family lunches complete the day. For the diaspora, community associations hold Puthandu cultural events the weekend closest to 14 April.

Tamil New Year Wishes (புத்தாண்டு வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

இனிய தமிழ் புத்தாண்டு நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Iniya Tamil Puthandu Nalvazhthukkal — "Happy Tamil New Year!"

புத்தாண்டு நல்ல ஆண்டாக அமைய வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Puthandu nalla aanduhaaga amaiya vaazhthukkal — "Wishing that the new year turns out to be a good one."

சித்திரை திங்கள் நல்ல திங்கள்

Chithirai thingal nalla thingal — "Chithirai is a good month": a traditional saying used in new-year greetings.

Government Holiday Notes for Puthandu 2027

Puthandu on Wednesday, 14 April 2027 is expected to be a Tamil Nadu government holiday; many private offices also close for the day, and some give 13 April as well to create a longer break. Schools are usually on summer schedule around the festival. As the holiday falls midweek, check office calendars early and book any hometown travel in advance.

Auspicious Notes for the New Year

Chithirai, the month that opens with Puthandu, is among the most auspicious of the year: property registrations, business openings and wedding muhurtham bookings surge in the weeks that follow. The Madurai Chithirai festival also runs in this season. For exact muhurtham timings, always consult a panchangam and your family priest — see our muhurtham dates 2027 guide.
