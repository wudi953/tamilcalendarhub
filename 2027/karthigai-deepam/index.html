Karthigai Deepam 2027

The festival of lamps falls on Sunday, 12 December 2027 — when rows of clay lamps light up Tamil homes and the great flame rises atop Arunachala hill at Tiruvannamalai.

Date: Sunday, 12 December 2027
Tamil name: கார்த்திகை தீபம் (Karthigai Deepam)
Iconic event: Maha Deepam at Tiruvannamalai
Season: Karthigai month lamps all month

All dates in IST, calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary.

Karthigai Deepam 2027 Date and Time

Karthigai Deepam falls on Sunday, 12 December 2027, on the full-moon day of the Karthigai month when the Karthigai star is in the ascendant. The most famous ritual is the Maha Deepam at Tiruvannamalai: a huge cauldron of ghee is lit atop the 2,600-foot Arunachala hill at the auspicious moment in the evening — traditionally around 6 pm, though the exact minute is announced by the temple each year from the panchangam. Millions gather for the ten-day Brahmotsavam leading up to it, and the beacon is visible for kilometres. At home, families light lamps (agal vilakku) at dusk, adding one more each evening through the month.

How Karthigai Deepam Is Celebrated

Karthigai is Tamil Nadu's own festival of lights, older than Deepavali and uniquely tied to the idea of Shiva as a column of fire. Through the month, families light clay lamps at the doorway each evening, and on Deepam day the whole house glows — lamps in windows, on kolam-drawn courtyards, along verandahs and balconies. New lamps are bought for the season, and it is considered auspicious to gift lamps to elders and newly married daughters.

In the kitchen, the day is marked with special preparations: pori urundai (puffed rice balls with jaggery), adhirasam, and other sweets are made in many households, and the lamps are placed with flowers and sandal paste. Shiva temples perform deepa aradhanai — the ceremonial waving of lamps — and Murugan temples connected to the Karthigai stars hold special pujas. At Tiruvannamalai, devotees circumnavigate the hill (girivalam) on the full-moon night, a path of about 14 kilometres completed barefoot by lakhs of pilgrims. Visitor notes: accommodation in Tiruvannamalai sells out weeks ahead, and approach roads are closed to vehicles on the day of the Maha Deepam.

Karthigai Deepam Wishes in Tamil (கார்த்திகை தீபம் வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

கார்த்திகை தீபம் நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Karthigai Deepam Nalvazhthukkal — "Happy Karthigai Deepam!"

இருள் காலத்தில் ஒளி விளக்கேற்றும் கார்த்திகை தீபம்!

Irul kaalathil oli vilakku yetrum Karthigai Deepam — "Karthigai Deepam, the lamp that lights the darkness."

வீடு விளக்கு ஒளியுடன் தீபம் நிறைய வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Veetu vilakku oliyudan theepam niraiva vaazhthukkal — "May your home glow with lamps this Deepam."

Government Holiday Notes for Karthigai Deepam 2027

Karthigai Deepam falls on Sunday, 12 December 2027 — a weekend, so no extra leave is needed for most people. The festival is widely observed in Tamil Nadu, and Tiruvannamalai authorities typically declare local arrangements; check the state gazette for the exact holiday status. Christmas on Saturday, 25 December follows two weeks later, rounding out the holiday season.

Auspicious Notes Around Karthigai Deepam

Early December, before Margazhi begins around 16 December, is a popular stretch for final muhurtham events of the year; Margazhi itself is traditionally avoided for weddings. Lamps lit on Karthigai Deepam are believed to bring prosperity for the year ahead, and gifting lamps to newly married daughters is a long-standing custom. For timings, see our muhurtham dates guide.
