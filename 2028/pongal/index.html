Pongal 2028

The four-day harvest festival of Tamil Nadu: Bhogi on 14 January, Pongal on 15 January, Mattu Pongal on 16 January and Uzhavar Thirunal on 17 January 2028.

Date: Saturday, 15 January 2028 (Thai 1)
Tamil name: தைப்பொங்கல் (Thai Pongal)
Season: Bhogi 14 · Pongal 15 · Mattu Pongal 16 · Uzhavar Thirunal 17
Expected holidays: Pongal 15 · Uzhavar Thirunal 17 (TN)

All dates in IST, calculated for Chennai — other regions may vary.

Pongal 2028 Date and Time

Pongal falls on Saturday, 15 January 2028, the first day of the Tamil month Thai, when the Sun enters Makara. The main household ritual — cooking the Pongal pot — is traditionally done in the auspicious part of the morning, after sunrise and the early puja. Families set up the decorated clay pot in the courtyard or verandah and boil newly harvested rice with jaggery and milk until it boils over with the cry "Pongalo Pongal!". Most celebrations run from sunrise through the morning; there is no single fixed minute for households, though priests may suggest a window from the panchangam.

How Pongal Is Celebrated

Pongal is the thanksgiving of the Tamil farming year, observed over four days. On Bhogi (14 January), houses are cleaned and painted and old belongings are burnt before sunrise on a small bonfire, a symbolic casting away of the old. On Pongal day itself (15 January), the household cooks the new rice: the decorated pot, often tied with fresh turmeric and ginger saplings, is set over a fire in the open, and the moment the milk boils over is greeted as a sign of prosperity. Surya, the Sun god, is offered the first share.

On Mattu Pongal (16 January), the cattle that ploughed the fields are bathed, their horns painted, and they are fed the first Pongal of the day. In villages around Madurai, Trichy and beyond, traditional bull events draw large crowds. The fourth day, Uzhavar Thirunal (17 January), honours the farmers who cultivate the land and is observed as a state holiday. Families spend these days visiting relatives, sharing sugarcane, ven pongal and payasam, and renewing kolams at the entrance.

Pongal Wishes in Tamil (பொங்கல் வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

பொங்கலோ பொங்கல்! இனிய தைப்பொங்கல் நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Pongalo Pongal! Iniya Thaipongal Nalvazhthukkal — "May this Pongal bring you a happy Thai Pongal!"

நல்ல அறுவடை, நிறைவான ஆண்டு நிறைய வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Nalla aruvadai, niraivaana aandu niraiva vaazhthukkal — "Wishing you a good harvest and a fulfilling year."

தை பிறந்தால் வழி பிறக்கும்

Thai pirandhal vazhi pirakkum — the classic proverb: "When Thai is born, a way opens" — the belief that the Thai month brings solutions and fresh beginnings.

Government Holiday Notes for Pongal 2028

Pongal on 15 January and Uzhavar Thirunal on 17 January are expected Tamil Nadu holidays, with Mattu Pongal on 16 January forming part of the same break. Because Pongal falls on a Saturday in 2028, the four days run across a weekend, which eases travel for many families. Schools, banks and most government offices close; confirm the final gazette before fixing leave. Travel is heavy, so reserve buses and trains well before mid-January.
