About This Calendar

2028 is a leap year, so February has 29 days. January carries the biggest festival of the Tamil year: Pongal falls on 15 January, with Bhogi, Mattu Pongal and Uzhavar Thirunal around it. Only the dates confirmed above are listed here; other festival timings will be added once they are confirmed against a panchangam.
