Deepavali 2028

Deepavali 2028 Date and Time

In Tamil Nadu, Deepavali is observed on Tuesday, 17 October 2028, following the Tamil panchangam convention for the festival. The most important timing of the day is the ritual oil bath (ganga snanam), taken before sunrise: elders apply oil to the younger members of the family before the bath, and everyone then dresses in new clothes. Crackers and visits run through the day, and lamps are lit after dusk. The North Indian Diwali date is not given here because it is computed from a different tithi and is outside the confirmed data for this calendar.

Why Tamil Deepavali Differs from Diwali

The Tamil panchangam computes the festival from its own tithi rather than from the Amavasya-based reckoning used in North India, so the two observances can fall on different days. Both honour the same core story: the defeat of the demon Narakasura, remembered as Naraka Chathurdasi, the day the tyrant was vanquished and the world freed from fear. The difference in date is a long-standing regional custom, not a disagreement. Tamil families follow the date announced in the Tamil panchangam and by local temple authorities, which for 2028 is 17 October.

How Deepavali Is Celebrated in Tamil Nadu

The Tamil Deepavali day starts before dawn. Elders apply gingelly (sesame) oil to the heads of the younger family members, followed by the hot water bath, often with a packet of shikakai powder. After the bath comes the first bite: a piece of medicine-like lehiyam, a herb-and-jaggery paste that elders prepare to protect the stomach through a day of sweets and fried snacks. The morning is spent in new clothes, temple visits and meeting neighbours.

Sweets and savouries are prepared days ahead: adhirasam, mixture, thattai, murukku and the classic Deepavali legiyam. Crackers begin softly in the morning and build to a loud evening, and city authorities issue fire-safety advisories for the season. Many families also perform a Lakshmi puja or visit their family temple, and it is customary to visit elders and receive their blessings first thing after the bath. For businesses, Deepavali is a natural milestone for closing old accounts and blessing new ones.

Deepavali Wishes in Tamil (தீபாவளி வாழ்த்துக்கள்)

தீபாவளி நல்வாழ்த்துக்கள்!

Deepavali Nalvazhthukkal — "Happy Deepavali!"

தீபாவளி நல்லது செய்யட்டும், ஒளி நிறைந்த ஆண்டாக அமையட்டும்!

Deepavali nalladhu seyyattum, oli niraaindha aandu aghaiyattum — "May Deepavali bring good, and may the year be filled with light."

ஒளியும் மகிழ்ச்சியும் நிறைந்த தீபாவளி!

Oliyum magizhchiyum niraaindha Deepavali — "A Deepavali filled with light and joy!"

Government Holiday Notes for Deepavali 2028

Deepavali on Tuesday, 17 October 2028 is expected to be a Tamil Nadu government holiday, and many companies add the following day to bridge into the weekend. Confirm the official gazette when it is notified. Travel peaks around the festival: buses and trains to hometowns sell out weeks ahead, and firecracker markets see their busiest weeks.

Auspicious Notes Around Deepavali

Deepavali sits in the Aippasi month, one of the favoured stretches for weddings and housewarmings in the Tamil year. Families often combine Deepavali visits with plans for ceremonies in the months that follow. Exact muhurtham timings are not given here, because a suitable moment depends on the family chart, the weekday and the tithi in the local panchangam. Ask a family priest before fixing an important date.
