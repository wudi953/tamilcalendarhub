Tamil Calendar 2028 February

February is a quiet month in the Tamil calendar. Thai continues until about the 13th and then Masi begins, with no festival dates confirmed for 2028. The city calendars are largely clear, so this page keeps the grid simple: 29 days because 2028 is a leap year. Temple routine continues through the cool mornings, and families use the gap between Pongal and the spring festivals for ordinary work, travel and small family occasions.

Government Holidays in February 2028

No Tamil Nadu holiday is confirmed for February 2028 in the current data, and no national holiday falls in the month. Banks, schools and government offices are therefore expected to follow normal hours throughout. Private employers usually do the same, although individual companies may add a local holiday. The official state gazette is published closer to the year, and that list is the one that matters for leave planning; treat this page as a guide only and check the gazette once it appears.

Auspicious Days and Notes

The month opens in Thai, the auspicious harvest month that began with Pongal, and moves into Masi around the 13th or 14th. Masi is traditionally linked with Shiva worship, and many devotees keep Monday vows and visit temples for abhishekam during this stretch. Families sometimes use these quieter weeks for engagements and small ceremonies, but no muhurtham timings are given here: an auspicious moment depends on the family chart, the tithi and the local panchangam. Ask a priest before fixing any important date.

What the Tamil Months Mean

Two Tamil months share February. Thai (தை), the month of the harvest festival, is read as the fresh or pure month and carries the momentum of Pongal into the first half. Masi (மாசி), which takes over around the 14th, is associated with the waning half of the lunar cycle and with Shiva traditions in Tamil temples. The switching point is approximate: only the starts of Thai on 15 January and Chithirai on 14 April are fixed for 2028, so this page marks the change as till ~13 and from ~14.

Travel and Temple Notes

February is one of the easier months for temple travel in Tamil Nadu. The Pongal rush is over, the mornings are cool, and the major pilgrimage crowds have not yet formed. Roads to village temples are usually clear, and booking buses or trains at short notice is often possible. Carry a light layer for early-morning darshan, remove footwear at the sanctum, and check local notices for maintenance work or small processions. Visitors from abroad should confirm visa and travel dates well ahead.
