Tamil Calendar 2028 November

Government Holidays in November 2028

Nothing in the confirmed 2028 data creates a state holiday in November, and there is no national holiday to note. Government offices, banks and schools are expected to keep their normal schedule. Private firms generally follow the same pattern, although some allow a floating leave after the October festival. Final leave planning should wait for the Tamil Nadu gazette, published closer to the year, which overrides any guide; this page records only what is confirmed today.

Auspicious Days and Notes

Aippasi, which continues until about the 13th, is counted among the favoured stretches for weddings and housewarmings, and Karthigai takes over around the 14th as the air cools. Families often use the month to settle ceremonies planned earlier in the year. No muhurtham timings are listed here: an auspicious moment depends on the family chart, the weekday and the tithi in the local panchangam. Ask a priest before fixing any important date.

What the Tamil Months Mean

November is shared by Aippasi (ஐப்பசி) and Karthigai (கார்த்திகை). Aippasi is named after the Apam star and is often read as a month of easing heat and deepening devotional life. Karthigai, which begins around the 14th, takes its name from the Karthigai stars and is associated in popular explanation with the cooling weather and the clearing skies of the north-east monsoon. The handover is shown as till ~13 and from ~14 because only the starts of Thai on 15 January and Chithirai on 14 April are fixed in the 2028 data; this boundary is approximate.

Seasonal Customs and Temple Routine

November is a quiet month of regular temple life. The same weekly pujas continue, with Monday abhishekam and evening lamps drawing steady crowds rather than festival masses, and the cooler air makes early-morning and late-evening visits comfortable. At home, families put away the Deepavali decorations, sort through the sweets tins, and return to ordinary routines. Small local donations and lamp offerings are common, but no dated observance is added here because none is confirmed for 2028.

Travel and Temple Notes

The post-Deepavali lull makes November a practical month for temple travel. Rain is still possible in the north-east monsoon, so check the forecast before a long drive and keep some flexibility in the plan. Queues are usually ordinary, and buses or trains can often be booked at short notice. Remove footwear at the entrance, follow the queue, and check local notices for maintenance work at the shrine.
