Tamil Calendar 2028 May

May follows the Tamil New Year, carrying the calendar from Chithirai into Vaikasi. No festival date is confirmed for the month in the current 2028 data, so the grid stays simple. The heat rises across Tamil Nadu, temple committees settle into the post-Puthandu routine, and the month is generally used for ordinary work, weddings and travel rather than one large public celebration.

The state gazette is published closer to the year and takes precedence over any guide, so use this page for early planning only and confirm the final list once it appears.

Auspicious Days and Notes

Chithirai, the first month of the Tamil year, continues until about the 13th and is widely regarded as favourable for new ventures, property matters and weddings. Vaikasi takes over around the 14th and is traditionally a month of temple worship and charitable giving. No muhurtham timings are listed here: an auspicious moment depends on the family chart, the weekday and the tithi in the local panchangam. Ask a priest before fixing any important date.

What the Tamil Months Mean

May is shared by Chithirai (சித்திரை) and Vaikasi (வைகாசி). Chithirai opens the Tamil year with Puthandu and carries the idea of a fresh start, new account books and decorated doorways. Vaikasi, which begins around the 14th, is associated in popular explanation with visiting temples and giving alms, and its name is linked to the moon. The changeover is marked as till ~13 and from ~14 because only the starts of Thai on 15 January and Chithirai on 14 April are fixed in the 2028 data; this boundary is approximate.

Travel and Temple Notes

May is hot across most of Tamil Nadu, so temple visits are best planned in the early morning or late evening. With no major festival in the confirmed list, queues are usually ordinary and road closures unlikely, which makes this a practical month for a short temple circuit. Carry water, a hat and light cotton clothing, and allow time to rest during the afternoon heat. Coastal routes can be humid, so check local conditions before a long drive.
